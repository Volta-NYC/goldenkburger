4 — Our Story

Who We Are

The crowned king of Queens burgers — built from two worlds, one kitchen.

Golden K Burgers is a cozy, Korean-American family-owned restaurant in Bayside, Queens. We serve a broad lineup of beef, veggie, seafood, and chicken burgers, each plate built for real appetite and consistency from first bite to last. Every burger is served with crispy fries, pepperoncini, and pickles, and guests can upgrade fries with favorites like Truffle Fries, Garlic Parmesan Fries, Sweet Potato Fries, and more.

Our kitchen is known for bold flavor and generous portions. The double-patty beef builds are designed to feel stacked, satisfying, and unmistakably Golden K. Alongside burgers, our menu includes brunch, weekday lunch deals, late-night options, desserts and milkshakes, appetizers, wings, beer, happy hour specials, and more, so there is always something for both first-time guests and regulars.

As a family-run small business, we focus on hospitality, quality, and staying close to the neighborhood that supports us. The goal is simple: make food worth coming back for and become a lasting local favorite in Queens.

Restaurant History

Golden K Burgers (GKB) celebrated its 2nd anniversary in May 2026, marking a strong early chapter built on consistency, repeat guests, and neighborhood support. Owned and operated by the Kim Family, Korean Americans who brought their culture, experience, and influence into the concept, GKB offers a distinct take on the classic American burger spot. With 20+ towering burger options and a reputation for bold flavor and generous portions, the restaurant has quickly built a loyal local following. The name Golden K comes directly from the family name, Kim: Kim is associated with gold, and the K represents family identity, connecting the brand back to its roots.
