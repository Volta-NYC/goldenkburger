# 🍔 Golden K Burgers — Website

Official website for **Golden K Burgers**, a Korean-American family-owned burger restaurant in Bayside, Queens, NY. Built and maintained by [Volta NYC](https://voltanyc.org/).

🌐 **Live site:** [goldenkburger.vercel.app](https://goldenkburger.vercel.app)

---

## About

Golden K Burgers (GKB) is owned and operated by the Kim family — Korean Americans who blended their culinary heritage with the classic American burger experience. The name comes from the family name itself: *Kim* means gold in Korean, and the *K* is for the family.

The restaurant is located at **40-17 Bell Blvd, Bayside, Queens, NY 11361** and celebrated its 2nd anniversary in May 2026.
